Started by students. Still run like a lab.

Beastfolk earn about a tenth of what humans earn for the same hours. Most work without contracts. One sentence from a manager can end a job, and a month’s wages with it.

What we believe.

Four rules we wrote as students and still use to make every decision.

A contract, not a favor.

Work without a contract can end with one sentence. Every placement we fund ends in writing.

Health before hiring.

Beastfolk become dependent faster than humans. We plan for it from the first appointment, not after the first relapse.

Measure everything.

We publish our outcomes, our costs and our criteria, including the ones we are not proud of.

Names are not paperwork.

Beastfolk change names at marriage and lose records with them. We help people get their own name back on file.

Our approachAbout beastfolk

Three ways back in.

Every program is built with employers, clinicians and beastfolk who have been through it.

Pathways to Work

Twelve weeks of paid training, a mentor, and a written contract at the end. Without a contract you can be let go with a sentence and never paid.

Health First

Clinic access for chronic pain, nutrition and dependency. Beastfolk become dependent faster than humans, so we plan for that from day one.

Papers & Housing

Legal help with visas, expired passports and leases, for those the system has stopped seeing. We'll help you find a place of your own, not just a bed.
